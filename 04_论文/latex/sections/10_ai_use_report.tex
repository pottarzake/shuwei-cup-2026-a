\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{AI Tool Use Report}

This report follows the contest AI-use rules. DeepSeek and OpenAI Codex were
used on 21 November 2026; only content confirmed by the team is recorded. Both
tools served as drafting assistants. The team retained responsibility for the
modeling decisions, for verifying every adopted number, and for the final
wording.

\paragraph{DeepSeek (DeepSeek AI; V4.1-Flash, released 2026-10-17).}
Documented use: 21 November 2026. Representative requests: implement and debug
the P1--P4 simulation chain; draft calibration, data-analysis and optimization
code; fit the production surrogate to the training groups of Attachment~C;
rerun the optimizer with the calibrated volume and composition responses.
Adopted content: the scripts and selected result files in the supporting
archive, together with generated plots and numerical outputs for pressure
redistribution, damage, production calibration and the optimization runs. Each
adopted script was read by the modeling and programming teammates; the
governing equations, units and boundary conditions were re-derived
independently before adoption.

\paragraph{Codex (OpenAI; GPT-6.2 Sol).}
Documented use: 21 November 2026. Representative requests: review and repair
the implementation; check the manuscript against the verification reports and
the contest requirements; prepare the submission files; draft the
fracturing-only B1 comparison and the break-even storage-length calculation.
Adopted content: reviewed code, the B1 comparison and break-even calculation
(the B1 values are calculations from the reduced production proxy), manuscript
and reference corrections, and the compiled-PDF and reproduction-package
checks. The writing teammate rewrote the adopted AI-generated text during final
editing.

\paragraph{Human review and independent verification.}
The core modeling, solution strategy and conclusions were completed
independently by the team; AI outputs served as drafts and were checked before
adoption. In particular, the team (i) derived the one-dimensional
fracture-normal reduction and selected the three-phase formulation with a free
CO$_2$ phase, the incremental poroelastic relations, the compressive-strain
damage law and the mirror-kernel scaling; (ii) re-implemented the Sobol
sampling design and total-order estimator (\texttt{sobol\_lib.py}) and
validated them on the Ishigami function and by replication of the forward
evaluation (agreement to $10^{-13}$); (iii) re-ran the grouped cross-validation
after aligning the feature set with the model outline (CV RMSE 25.1;
frozen-test MAPE 0.53\% and 3.59\%); (iv) reproduced the economic self-check of
the problem statement exactly ($848.25\times10^4$~CNY) and recomputed the B1
incremental NPVs from the packaged inputs; and (v) checked every bibliography
entry against the source documents. The B1 baseline remains a
fracturing-only counterfactual and still lacks independent production
observations.
